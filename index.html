Dual Participation Model

Two Structured Membership Categories

Designed for flexibility while keeping every weekly draw unit strictly equal at ₹100 per entry.

Category A

₹100 Individual Membership

Full independent participation. Each individual member contributes ₹100 per week and holds 1 dedicated entry in the weekly lucky draw.

  • Weekly contribution: ₹100 / week
  • Draw weight: 1 Independent Entry
  • Prize entitlement: 100% Full Draw Prize
Automatically excluded from duplicate wins once selected in the active cycle.
Category B

₹50 Paired Membership

Two ₹50 members are paired together to form a single ₹100/week unit. Both partners appear together and share the prize equally if drawn.

  • Combined weekly contribution: ₹50 + ₹50 = ₹100
  • Draw weight: 1 Combined Pair Entry
  • Prize entitlement: 50% / 50% Joint Split
Unpaired ₹50 members remain ineligible until paired by the administrator.
